Reference

Neck connection

Two 15 × 24 × 5 mm bearings, 16 mm between centers, support a proposed 15 mm shaft with a 10 mm cable passage. The head flange rests on a shaft shoulder. A lower collar, upper spacer, and removable keeper reserve axial retention. The upright servo sits toward the front in a removable body cradle and drives a proposed 1:1 gear pair. The circles show drive space, not finished gear teeth. Joint outlines are simplified; the STEP assembly includes the spacer, collar, and keeper.

Load path: head flange, shaft shoulder, bearing inner rings, bearing outer rings, housing, and body frame. The servo horn uses its supplied retaining screw. Electronics install into edge-supported cradles with removable snap catches; final catches are not modeled yet.

Assembly and release access

Remove the rear body cover, install the edge-supported Jetson cradle, and connect power and USB cables through the side service opening. Install the speaker and USB audio unit from the front. Install the servo and bearing module from the open top. A side-loading split shaft must let complete camera connectors enter without cutting cables. Load the camera from the open rear of the head, engage its edge clamp, leave the service loop loose, and close the rear head cover.

The shell outlines are packaging envelopes. Rounded walls, vents, latches, stops, strain relief, and mating fits are future geometry. This file is not a print release.
