# Concept A casing and assembly prototype

Build a removable casing and manually checked joint prototype around the owned STS3215 layout. Preserve 136 × 112 × 160 mm body and 230 mm overall envelope where mounts fit. Enlarge local features if a real clearance needs it. Final powered operation requires physical fits and verified hardware interfaces.

## Alternatives

A removable rear cover plus full port-side panel provides straight insertion access. A two-piece clamshell gives wider access but splits more structural mounts. Compare component insertion sweeps, part count, first layers, and access to catches. Use removable rear and side panels for this prototype.

A split hollow shaft preserves the proposed bearing-supported neck and hidden cable route. External camera routing simplifies installation but needs protected movement space. Both closed bearing bores must admit the complete connector. A split shaft alone does not provide this access. Use external routing for manual tests until connector admission and physical cable motion are verified.

## Required geometry

Create flat-printable body casing panels, structural roof/neck carrier, rear head cover, component cradle prototypes, shaft halves, outer-race retention, shaft collars, and a real involute gear pair. Keep all electronics contact regions provisional unless the reference specifies an unpopulated edge. Reserve both source camera rear connectors and heatsink. Keep fan and outlet reservations open.

The Jetson socket plane is provisional X37.75. Its installed power elbow begins 11 mm outward. An ordinary side wall at X68 is too far away. Use a removable trial insert with its exterior face 9 mm from the socket plane, at provisional X46.75. Use a 13 mm edge-open channel for the reported 11 mm plug diameter. The rotatable elbow points upward for this test. Remove the entire insert to connect or grip the plug. Exact elbow geometry, socket datums, and other USB plug shapes remain pending. Test these interfaces before combining the insert with the casing.

Provide separate stationary bearing outer-race capture and rotating inner-race spacers. The former ID24 keeper cannot retain an OD24 bearing. The prototype lip must overlap the outer race while clearing the shaft spacer. Check both axial contacts and radial clearance before manual assembly. Verify contact lands against the actual bearing before powered use.

## Acceptance

Deliver the removable power-exit trial first. Check its CAD validity, closed 3MF volume, millimeter scale, bed fit, and flat base. Check the modeled plug against the complete insert during upward assembly and final seating. Check the insert against the Jetson envelope. Export its STEP assembly, dimensions, and instructions. This isolated trial provides no USB-port, cable-flex, or final casing validation.

For the subsequent casing and joint delivery, check assembled part intersections, hardware separation, complete insertion sweeps, both lens focus limits, and actual gear mesh. Declare mating contacts explicitly. Label fit parameters and physical unknowns. Test provisional cradles with gauges first. Install electronics only after verifying supported contacts and accessible releases. Inspect and slice each prototype in Bambu Studio before marking it ready to print.

Stop before powering a provisional interface or claiming final hardware fit. The deliverable is a useful mechanical prototype for measurements and fit tests.
