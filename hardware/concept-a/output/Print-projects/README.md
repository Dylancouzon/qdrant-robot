# P1S print projects

Open the native 3MF projects in Bambu Studio. Required contains 38 installed pieces: 17 White and 21 Black. Optional contains 19 gauges and coupons: 14 White and five Black.

These are unchanged copies from `../bambu/`. They retain checked orientations, sliced previews, and P1S settings: PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, three walls, and automatic tree supports. Inspect supports and test coupons before printing. Physical fit and retention remain unverified.
