Assembly guide

This is an unpowered mechanical fit prototype. CAD checks are E (verified on the Mac). Physical fits and retention remain U (unverified).

Test the panel coupons and spare keys first. Check safe support lands before installing populated electronics. Inspect every print in Bambu Studio.

  1. Body and cradles. Remove the roof, rear cover, and right panel. Fit the speaker tray, audio shelf, and Jetson tray onto their supports.
  2. Stationary components. Slide the speaker through the open rear at its final height. Fit the audio housing. Check servo admission with a dummy envelope before placing the Jetson. Keep the owned servo separate.
  3. Body panels and roof. Connect and grip body plugs with the right panel removed. Fit the rear cover and right panel, then seat the roof on its four posts. Keep the rear roof channel open.
  4. Manual joint. Slide the lower bearing, middle spacer, and upper bearing onto the gear shaft. Close both tower halves around them. Fit four cross pins and four keepers. Hold the joint 5.2 mm high, slide it through the rear roof channel, then lower it onto the posts. Fit both roof keys. Seat the detachable head flange on the D-flat and fit its transverse shaft key.
  5. Bridge and neck cover. Slide the roof bridge forward beneath the seated flange. Fit two bridge pins and two keepers. Check their retention. Fit the neck cover before installing the head.
  6. Head and camera. Lower the head onto the flange posts. Fit both head keys through the open rear. Insert the empty camera frame from the rear, 1.5 mm above its final height, then lower it onto its posts. Insert the complete camera through the frame after checking safe edge support.
  7. Covers and connections. Connect the camera with the rear head cover open. Route its cable externally through the split rear exits. Fit the visor and rear head cover. Check cable slack through manual head travel before any powered design.

Connections

Connect the camera USB cable and USB audio module to the Jetson. Connect the speaker to the audio module after checking the owned connectors. Confirm microphone positions.

The proposed powered path uses Jetson USB, a compatible TTL bus-servo adapter, and the STS3215. Adapter identity, supply wiring, grounding, horn retention, and installed servo mounting remain pending.

Service access

Remove the head, neck cover, roof bridge, joint, and roof before removing body panels. Remove the rear cover first for access to the right rear sockets. Pull each panel along its insertion direction.

Remove the head before lifting the neck cover 60 mm over the retained mechanism. Release accessible key barbs before withdrawing keys. Keep cables clear during removal.

Full references: casing assembly and joint assembly. These files are included beside the offline preview directory.
