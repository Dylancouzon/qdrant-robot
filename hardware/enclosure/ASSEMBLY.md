# Assembly

Assemble the robot from nine printed parts, 17 M3 screws, four M2 screws, and 13 M3 nuts. The servo kit also supplies its own horn screw. The fastener list is in [README.md](README.md). Physical fit stays U (unverified) until you print and test the coupon plate.

Tools: a 2.5 mm hex key, a 2.5 mm ball-end hex key, and a 2.5 mm short-arm L-key. You also need a driver for the M2 screws, tweezers, a soft cloth, and four blocks about 40 mm tall.

## Before you start

- Print the coupon plate first. Fit one M3 nut in each of its two channels. Each nut must snap past the 0.3 mm ridge and stay.
- Thread one M3 screw through each coupon hole. Thread an M2 screw into each camera post.
- The hub is the bearing coupon. The horn disc is the horn coupon. Test both before you print the large parts.

## Assemble

1. **Press the bearings into the hub.** Press the lower bearing in from below. Press the upper bearing in from above. Press only on the outer race. Both bearings stop on the center shoulder.
2. **Fit the corner nuts.** Turn the shell so its opening faces you. Slide one M3 nut into each of the four corner channels. Keep the flats along the channel walls. Each nut clicks past its ridge and stays.
3. **Fit the hub nuts, then screw the hub to the roof.** Stand the shell roof up on the four blocks. Drop three M3 nuts through the 32 mm roof hole with tweezers. Push each nut sideways into its channel until it clicks past the ridge. Set the hub on the roof. Drive three M3 × 16 screws down through the hub with the 2.5 mm hex key.
4. **Fix the horn disc to the servo horn.** Plug the servo into the bus board, and center it with the bus board software before you fit the horn. The horn has 14.4° spline steps. Set the horn so the head will face forward. Fix the horn with the servo kit's own screw. Drop one M3 nut into the pocket under the disc. Set the disc on the horn. Drive four M3 × 6 screws through the disc with the 2.5 mm hex key.
5. **Insert the servo from below.** Slide the servo up into its pocket under the roof, with the disc entering the roof hole. Rest the servo on a block until the center screw is in. The two long pocket walls stop the servo from turning.
6. **Lower the shaft and drive the center screw.** Lower the shaft through both bearings until its square socket meets the disc. Drive one M3 × 16 screw down the shaft bore into the disc nut. The screw head sits 9.5 mm below the flange top. The shaft keeps 0.5 mm of axial play, so do not overtighten. Remove the block under the servo.
7. **Screw on the head.** Slide two M3 nuts into the flange channels from the flange rim. Lower the head shell onto the flange, with the front open and the visor off. Drive two M3 × 8 screws down through the head floor into the nuts. Use the 2.5 mm ball-end key through the front opening at about 20°.
8. **Fit the camera and the visor.** Slide an M3 nut into each visor tab slot from the rear of the visor. Screw the camera onto the four visor posts with M2 × 8 screws. Plug the camera USB cable into the camera. Route the cable out of the slot in the head's lower rear edge. Press the visor into the head front. Drive two M3 × 8 screws up through the head floor lip into the tab nuts. Use the 2.5 mm short-arm L-key from below.
9. **Fill the base plate.** Set each part loosely in its pocket. Each pocket leaves about 1 mm of clearance on each side (0.55 mm front and back for the bus board), and nothing presses in. The Jetson stands on edge with its ports toward the port side. Set the speaker, the USB audio case, the bus board, and the servo regulator in their pockets.
10. **Close the shell on the base plate.** Set the speaker grille into the front window from inside the shell. Hold the shell 60 mm above the base plate. Plug the servo lead into the bus board, with enough slack for this lift. Lower the shell onto the plate. Tip the robot onto its back on the cloth. Drive four M3 × 10 screws up through the plate counterbores into the corner nuts.
11. **Connect the cables.** Work through the open port window. Plug the camera USB into a Jetson USB port. The cable loops outside the robot and enters through the skirt notch. Plug the audio USB and the bus board USB into the Jetson. Connect the speaker lead to the audio case. Connect the servo power through the regulator. The regulator type stays U. Route the Jetson power cable through the skirt notch.
12. **Fit the port panel.** Drop one M3 nut into the slot in the base plate ear. Set the port panel in the window. Drive one M3 × 12 screw through the panel with the 2.5 mm hex key.

## Service

Service is the same steps in reverse. Remove the head first, then the shaft, then the shell.

- Remove the port panel screw. Pull the panel straight out from the wall.
- Unplug the camera USB at the Jetson. Unscrew the two visor screws from below, and pull the visor.
- Remove the two head screws through the front opening. Lift the head off the flange.
- Remove the center screw. Lift the shaft out of the bearings.
- Tip the robot onto its back, and remove the four plate screws. Lift the shell 60 mm, and unplug the servo lead. Lift the shell off the plate.
- The servo stays in its pocket above the Jetson. It cannot drop past the Jetson, but support it when you lift the shaft.

## Open items

- Camera cable length, bend radius, and strain relief stay U.
- The fit of the servo kit's horn screw under the disc stays U until you test the horn disc.
- Cooling, acoustics, and powered motion stay U until printed tests.
