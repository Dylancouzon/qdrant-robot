- F1 API qualification: Edge0.8 installed stubs expose native create_dense_vector. The design reviewer found that duplicate `[v,v]` cannot prove later-unit recall. Archive2026-10-09-f1-schema-distinct.py.txt and API JSONL preserve proof. This removes the assumption that every named-vector addition needs a separate shard or full migration. New multivector/retention/scope choices still need design and Jetson checks.

Keep show paths relative to the fixture, validate timed data through its source-aware manifest check, and retain legacy audio assertions. Raw cache verification belongs in preparation, so ordinary checks work without ignored downloads (D91).

- Judge variance and llama-server variance: identical prompts can give different answers; compare paired runs, use the judge cache, and do not read a 1 to 3 step change as real.
- Mac llama.cpp is not the robot: the Mac Homebrew build matched the Jetson on 51/93; the 3080 box at the Jetson's commit with GGML_CUDA_NSM=8 matches 63/93 (D75). Rank brains on the box; accept on the Jetson.
- Real-time replays are slow (5 to 15 min each) and serialize on the Jetson GPU lock: inspect prior results, iterate in text mode on the touched sessions, and run the full audio set once after a material change.
