Things you can sayAsk what Edgy sees, what you explicitly tracked earlier, or what you discussed. Show one object and say “Track this as my wallet” or “This is my phone”. Only named objects keep visual memories. Conversation memory stores text. Ask “Where is my phone?” or “Forget my phone”. Choose an item in the camera card and press “Do not track that” to ignore it; “Restore” brings it back. Ask Edgy to remember your face; it asks for consent first. Say “Forget me” to delete it. The robot ignores its microphone while its speaker plays and while muted; Mute and the volume slider act on the robot’s own USB microphone and speaker.
