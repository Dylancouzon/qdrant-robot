Talk with Edgy

Your MacBook carries the audio. The Jetson sees, listens, answers, and remembers.

Idle

The microphone starts when you select Start.

Say “Hey Edgy” to begin. You can ask follow-up questions for about 20 seconds after each answer. The microphone sends silence during playback and while muted. Stop releases the audio session; this page stays ready for next time.

The Mac stores no microphone recording. The Jetson keeps its existing conversation and answer logs.
