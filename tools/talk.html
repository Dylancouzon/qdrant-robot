HelpAll models and Qdrant Edge run on the Jetson. Ask what Edgy sees, what you explicitly tracked earlier, or what you discussed. Show one object and say “Track this as my wallet” or “This is my phone”. Only named objects keep visual memories. Conversation memory stores text. Ask “Where is my phone?” or “Forget my phone”. Choose an item in the camera card and press “Do not track that” to ignore it; “Restore” brings it back. Ask Edgy to remember your face; it asks for consent first. Say “Forget me” to delete it. Follow-up questions stay open for about a minute after each answer finishes. Say “Track this” with no name and Edgy asks what to call it. The robot ignores its microphone while its speaker plays and while muted; Mute and the volume slider act on the robot’s own USB microphone and speaker. Stop stops the robot on the Jetson and clears this view. Closing this page or losing the connection leaves the robot running; Start attaches to it again.
