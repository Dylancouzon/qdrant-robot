   I reproduced this with the tracked `_store()` method and stub storage:

   - At 100 seconds, “This is my camera” creates the object link.
   - At 130 seconds, “I use it for work” links correctly.
   - At 165 seconds, “I purchased it in Berlin” retains its statement vector but receives no object link.

   These turns have gaps under 60 seconds. Nevertheless, deleting the camera leaves the Berlin statement searchable through `Memory.told()` (`robot/memory.py:494-498`). Removing answer payloads does not remove that vector (`robot/main.py:2404-2408`).

   **Fix:** Keep the object association through active follow-ups. Refresh its timestamp when linking a statement, and clear it when the conversation closes or changes visitors.
